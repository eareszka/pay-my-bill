Support

Get help

Email support@yearnly.app. A person reads every message, usually within two days.

Report a post or a person

Tap Report at the bottom of any post or profile. An admin reviews every report. To stop seeing someone, tap Block on their profile or post. You can unblock them in Settings.

Money

Yearnly never holds app payments. Cash App, Venmo, and PayPal gifts go straight between people, so a payment problem goes to that app's support. Card gifts go through Stripe.

Your account

Reset a forgotten password from Log in. You can delete your account any time in Settings, which removes your profile and posts.
