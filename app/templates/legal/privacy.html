Privacy Policy

Draft, last updated {{ LEGAL_UPDATED }}. Pending legal review.

What we collect

  • Account: your username, your email, a hashed password (we never see the password itself), and when you confirmed you're 18 or older.
  • Profile: your Cash App, Venmo, or PayPal handles, and an optional city and state.
  • What you post: posts, results updates, reactions, the gifts you record, and reports.
  • Security: login attempts and IP addresses, which are used to stop password guessing.

What's public

Your username, posts, updates, payment handles (so people can pay you), giving stats, and city and state if you added them. Your email is never shown.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking cookies. We never see your bank or card details. App payments happen in Cash App, Venmo, or PayPal, and card payments and payouts happen at Stripe, which follows its own privacy policy.

Cookies

The only cookie is the one that keeps you logged in.

Who else handles it

Our host stores the site and database, an email provider sends our emails, and Stripe processes card gifts and recipient payouts. They handle data only to run the service.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account from your profile. That removes your profile and handles and scrubs your posts. Gift records stay without your details, so other people keep their credit. To ask about your data, email {{ CONTACT_EMAIL }}.

Age

Pay My Bill is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger.
