Who can use Pay My Bill

You must be 18 or older and live in the United States. One account per person. You're responsible for what happens on your account, so keep your password to yourself.

What Pay My Bill is

Pay My Bill is a bulletin board. People post everyday wants and needs, and others can choose to chip in. Pay My Bill never holds your money. It takes no cut of app gifts and a small fee on card gifts (below). App gifts go straight from the giver to the poster through Cash App, Venmo, or PayPal, under that app's own terms. Card gifts are paid straight into the poster's own Stripe account.

Card gifts

Card, Apple Pay, and Google Pay gifts are processed by Stripe. The giver pays exactly the amount they enter. Stripe's card fee (about {{ card_fee.card_pct }} + {{ card_fee.card_fixed }}¢, more for cards from outside the US) and a {{ card_fee.platform_pct }} Pay My Bill platform fee come out of each card gift, and the recipient gets the rest. Payment processing services for recipients are provided by Stripe and are subject to the Stripe Connected Account Agreement, which includes the Stripe Terms of Service. By taking card gifts, you agree to it, and you let Pay My Bill share your information and transactions with Stripe. Refunds and card disputes come out of the recipient's Stripe balance. Pay My Bill may refund a card gift that was fraud or a mistake.

Gifts are gifts

A gift is voluntary and final. It isn't a loan, purchase, investment, or charitable donation, and it isn't tax-deductible. Posts aren't verified. Pay My Bill doesn't check that a need is real or that money is used as described, and can't get a gift back for you. Chip in only what you're comfortable giving away, at your own risk.
