How Pay My Bill works

I want to help Chip in on someone's bill, straight from your own Venmo, Cash App, or PayPal. See how it works I need a hand Ask for up to $1,000: rent coming up short, a bill that's due, or just a game you want. See how it works

Pay My Bill never holds your money. Gifts go straight from one person's pay app to the other's.

How helping works

  1. Pick one person

    Asks come one at a time, full screen. Swipe to the next, swipe back, and stop on someone you want to help.

    1 of 4

  2. Pay them in your own app

    Pick an amount and your Venmo, Cash App, or PayPal opens to their page. The money goes straight to them.

    2 of 4

  3. They confirm it came through

    Tap "I sent it", and they see your gift waiting. They check their app. Only gifts they confirm count, so the credit is honest.

    3 of 4

  4. You get credit and a thank-you

    Once they confirm, your gift earns XP, levels, and badges, and counts toward the leaderboard. It counts people helped, never dollars.

    4 of 4

How asking works

  1. Ask for up to $1,000

    Say what it's for in a few lines: a need or just a want. Never for a business, a startup, or an investment.

    1 of 3

  2. People chip in through your app

    Gifts land in your own Cash App, Venmo, or PayPal. You check, then confirm what arrived.

    2 of 3

  3. Say how it went

    When your post closes, share a short update within 7 days. Posters who follow through get first shot at the top of the feed.

    3 of 3
