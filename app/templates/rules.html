Community Rules

  1. Everyday wants and needs only. Rent, bills, groceries, a game you've been wanting. Goals are capped at {{ MAX_GOAL }}.
  2. No business fundraising. No startups, products, inventory, pre-orders, investments, or anything that promises a return. Posts like that go to review and get removed.
  3. Be honest. Tell the real story. Your post history is visible, and scam posts get you banned.
  4. Be kind. No harassment, hate, or pressure, in posts or in thank-yous.
  5. Gifts, not loans. Money goes straight from the person who sent it to you through Cash App, Venmo, or PayPal. Yearnly never holds money, and people who chip in get nothing back but thanks.
  6. {{ MAX_OPEN_POSTS }} open posts at a time. Finish or close one before you post again.
  7. Follow through. After a post closes with gifts, share how it went within 7 days. Posters with a good record get first shot at the top of the board, and an overdue update blocks new posts.
